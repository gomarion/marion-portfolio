This site is my personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms and no ads, and I don't sell or share data about visitors. This page explains the little that is collected, and how you can control it.

## Visit counts and page speed (always on)

I use **Vercel Web Analytics** to count visits: which pages are viewed, the site that linked to them, and the visitor's country, device type and browser. It doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't record anything that identifies you personally.

I also use **Vercel Speed Insights**, which measures how quickly pages load for real visitors, so I can keep the site fast. It doesn't use cookies either.

## Google Analytics (only if you accept)

If you choose **Accept** in the cookie banner, the site also loads **Google Analytics 4**, which helps me understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages they read and how long they stay. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser (named `_ga` and `_ga_` followed by an ID) that last up to two years, so it can tell repeat visits apart. Google Analytics doesn't log or store your IP address.

If you choose **Decline**, or never answer, Google Analytics doesn't load and no Google cookies are set. You can change your mind at any time from **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page. Declining after accepting switches Google Analytics off and deletes its cookies.

Your answer itself is saved in your browser's local storage (as `analytics-consent`), so the banner doesn't ask again on every visit. Clearing your browser's site data resets it.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by **Vercel**. Like any web host, Vercel processes basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks.

## Emailing me

If you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply to you.
